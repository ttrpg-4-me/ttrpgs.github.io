Of course I want to play psychedelic [Oregon Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oregon_Trail_(series)?wprov=sfti1).
This is an amazingly creative setting book with really neat procedures for traveling across a strange land in a wagon.

There's a couple reasons why my "want to play" score is so low:

1. I don't really like the underlying system

There's a small system in the book that's basically a slightly stripped down dnd 5e. 5e is maybe my least favorite system. There's a community made Cairn conversion guide online, but doing that conversion requires some work and the smell doesn't totally scrub off.
I do still love the travel and camp procedures.

2. I'm having trouble eating the book

There's a ton of creative ideas here, but every time I sit down to read it I only make it about 40 pages. Then I put it down for a few months and forget everything.

This is definitely a book where you need to read the whole thing cover to cover a couple times to really "get" the setting. As you start reading it will immediately start referring to concepts like "ka-zombies", "V.I.L.E", "aerolith", "Decapolitan artists", etc. It's a hard read because you're immediately thrown into the deep end on a highly complex setting that you have no reference for. There's a glossary in the back of the book that's usually helpful, but there's so much weird and whimsical shit in this book that it's a challenge to remember enough of it to understand any individual page of this book without constantly flipping around to try to figure out what something means. I want to run this setting, but I don't want to invest so much effort that it feels like learning a new language. Even if I did wrap my head around the setting I imagine I'd have a really difficult time running the game without constantly pausing to explain shit to the players.
