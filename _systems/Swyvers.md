Your favorite grotty British heist game!

I love this game. There's something really freeing about telling your players that all they own is a shiv and rotting clothes. It really sets a "I have nothing to lose" tone that leads to some wild gameplay.

Reading the book is such a joy.
- There's a magic system where you play blackjack to see if spells succeed or backfire. The GM is instructed to tell players that magic doesn't exist before explaining the rules for casting spells.
- There's a 1/1000 chance that any dog you meet can talk, but the book adds that a talking dog probably wouldn't want to chat with a bastard like you.
