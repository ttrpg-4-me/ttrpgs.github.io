I **love** this system, here are a couple of the big reasons why:

## Dice Pool

Roll a fistfull of D6s, if any are a 6 you succeed. I love it because it's so low friction. I'm the type of person that hates doing a bunch of counting, there's a moment of anticipation that leads up to a roll that's lost when you need to stop and check modifiers and perform addition. You've transitioned out of the storytelling for a brief moment to do math homework, it costs momentum and it sucks.

This dice pool system requires one number lookup that's clearly visible on your character sheet (some skill that tells you how many d6s to roll). That lookup is prior to the roll, so there's still the same tension when the dice drop out of your hands. The tension resolves when, after a moment of desparate scanning across your dice pool, you find (or don't find) a 6. It's that easy and the pace never suffers. It's even easier if you take a blank 6 sided die and color one side green.

## Hidden Roles

this is a hidden roll game. Every player at the table has a hidden motivation. It naturally drives conflict and mystery at the table, and I love it so much.

It creates constant paranoia. Is that player working for a rival megacorp? Is that other player an android? Probably!
