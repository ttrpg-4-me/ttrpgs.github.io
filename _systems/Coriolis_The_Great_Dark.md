It's like a steampunk space survival/exploration game with lots of alien artifacts and a super interesting setting.

This game collects dust on my shelf. I've skimmed it and the premise seems cool and I love a dice pool system. I just have to sit down and read that whole damn book and then find a table.

## The Premise

(at least this is what I want the premise to be, haven't read the whole thing)

A religious cult escapes a galaxy at war in a generation ship. They're following a strange signal from deep space that's probably god. Whoopsie, it's now been X generations, the broadcast has stopped and we've used too much fuel to turn around. We're doomed to float through the wreckage of a long lost civilization, pilfering incomprehensible technology in a desperate attempt to find resources to keep life support online.

Does the alien orb change our culture's entire perspective on the origin of life? Yes. Is it food? No. Is it fuel? No. I guess we should just leave it behind then.
