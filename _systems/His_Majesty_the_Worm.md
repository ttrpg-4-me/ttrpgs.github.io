There's so much I love about this one. Much of what I think about this game is represented in [this wonderful blog post](https://personable.blog/qlbb-his-majesty-the-worm/).

Ultimately what's keeping me from jumping into this one head first is that I feel like I'd have to commit to a high effort campaign of this to get the most out of it, and there's a significant required player commitment as well. The tarot mechanics are awesome, but potentially difficult for players to learn and remember. Ultimately if a group of players approached me and really wanted to try this one that would be awesome, but I don't see myself pitching this game anytime soon.
