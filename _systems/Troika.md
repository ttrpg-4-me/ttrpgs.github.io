It's a really easy to use OSR system for psychedelic sci-fi fantasy stuff.

## The Flavor

There's so much of it in such a tiny package. The setting is so strange and fantastical that it's hard to figure out what the hell is going on. You might roll on a random table during character creation and finding out you're a "Befouler of Ponds". What does that mean? It's up to you and your players to figure that out at the table.

## The Modules

There's an expansive ecosystem of really great Troika modules. Some of my favorites are `The Moon King Wants to Party` and `The Hand of God`.

## The Art

The book itself is one of my favorite books to look at and use at the table. The art is weird and perfect for the setting.
