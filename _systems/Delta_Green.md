It's Call of Cthulhu, but better!
- Set in present day so everything feels more real and horrifying.
- There's lots of quality of life improvements
  - ex. All that normal/hard/extreme success math is gone, now it's normal success and crit if both tens and ones dice get the same number. Much easier to manage!
- Bonds - outside relationships that make you feel connected to the world. Watch those relationships fall to pieces as the horrors warp your mind!
