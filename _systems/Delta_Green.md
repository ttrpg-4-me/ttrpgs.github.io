It's Call of Cthulhu, but it's better for a couple reasons:
- set in present day so everything feels more real and horrifying
- quality of life improvements to rolls (ex. all that normal/hard/extreme success math is gone, now it's normsl success and crit if both tens and ones dice get the same number)
- Bonds - outside relationships that make you feel connected to the world
